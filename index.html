Clinical tools

Calculators built for the treatment room

Free tools on desmoVet that take the manual math out of dosing, CRIs, and fluid plans. Everything runs in the browser, and every one is live.

Small Animal Anesthesia Calculator

23 drugs · CRIs · protocol sheets

Weight-based dosing with ranges, CRI rates including dopamine and dobutamine, a feline ketamine–dexmedetomidine–opioid reference, and a printable protocol sheet from premedication through local blocks.

Built so a dose or CRI rate is one weight entry away during a busy shift.

Open the calculator ↗

EquiCalc

8 modules · fluids · GIR · TPN

Equine drug and fluid calculators: fluid deficits, shock and hypertonic boluses, glucose infusion rate, TPN formulation, lidocaine and MLK CRIs, induction and TIVA, and standing sedation.

Built for equine critical care, where neonatal fluid, GIR, and CRI math comes up constantly.

Bloodwork Abnormals

CBC · chem · gas · UA · coags

Paste raw analyzer output and get one sentence listing only the abnormal values, ready for the medical record.

Runs entirely in the browser, so no patient data is uploaded anywhere.
